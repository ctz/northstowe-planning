# Land West of Station Road, Longstanton — Section 106 Obligations, Contributions & Triggers

Summary of the s106 Deed (Town & Country Planning Act 1990, s.106) for **land west of Station Road, Longstanton, Cambridge CB24 3DS** dated 2022, between **South Cambridgeshire District Council (1)**, **Cambridgeshire County Council (2)**, the **Savvas Kides family — the First Owners (3)**, and **Longstanton Parish Council as Trustee of the charity known as Allotments for Public Stone, Gravel and Clay Pits — the Second Owner (4)**. Application ref **20/03598/OUT**.

> **Source / naming note:** Distilled from `bellway-s106.md`. Despite the filename, **no party named "Bellway" or "Homes England" appears in this deed** — the landowners are the Savvas Kides family and Longstanton Parish Council (the "Gravel Pits" site, promoted by Endurance Estates). This is a different agreement from the Phase 3B deed (ref 20/02142/OUT). The development is **up to 107 dwellings** (40% affordable) plus employment/community/cafe floorspace.

Key recurring terms:
- **Occupation** = occupation for permitted purposes (excluding construction/fit-out/marketing/display/security); "Occupy" construed accordingly.
- **Dwelling** = a dwelling constructed pursuant to the Planning Permission and any reserved matters approval.
- **Owners** = the First Owners (their land) and the Second Owner (its land), jointly and severally liable.
- **Requisite Number of Affordable Dwellings** = 40% of Dwellings (70% Affordable Rent / 30% shared-ownership Lease).
- Contributions are **Index Linked** (BCIS All-In Tender Price / General Building Cost Index), generally from the date of the deed unless a base quarter is specified (e.g. Education from 1Q2020, Library from 1Q2019).
- Triggers are mostly expressed as a **percentage of Dwellings Occupied** (≈107 total: 50% ≈ 53–54; 75% ≈ 80; 80% ≈ 86).
- "Council" = South Cambridgeshire **District** Council; "County Council" = Cambridgeshire **County** Council.
