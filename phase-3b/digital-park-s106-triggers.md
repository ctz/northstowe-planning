# Digital Park, Station Road, Longstanton — S106 triggers, contributions and obligations

Summary of the s106 deed dated **22 June 2023** (S/3854/19/OL, Middlereach Limited / South Cambridgeshire DC / Cambridgeshire CC) — see [digital-park-s106.md](digital-park-s106.md) for the full text.

Development: outline permission for **up to 80 dwellings** on ~2.38 ha. Percentage triggers below are percentages of Dwellings (or of Market Dwellings where stated); indicative counts assume the full 80.

All sums are **index-linked** (BCIS or CPI) from the base dates in clause 10 — the cash figures below are base values, not the sums actually payable.

## Affordable housing requirements (ongoing)

- **33% of Dwellings** affordable (≈26 of 80), split **70% Affordable Rent / 30% shared ownership Lease** (rounded per the Council's Affordable Housing SPD).
- **Clustering**: no more than **15** Affordable Dwellings per cluster unless agreed.
- Occupation limited to **Qualifying Persons** in South Cambridgeshire; after 8 weeks with no qualifying taker, the RP may let to others.
- **Nomination rights**: initial allocation of each Affordable Rented Dwelling — Council 100%; thereafter Council 75% / RP 25% (Fourth Schedule).
- Shared ownership Leases: ≥990 years, initial share 10–75%, staircasing to 100% permitted.
- **Orchard Land**: minimum 412 m² within the Site (shortfall against policy compensated by the Compostable Toilet Contribution).

## Clawback

Contributions not applied (or arrangements made to apply them) **within 10 years** of receipt become repayable to the payer with interest at 4% over NatWest base rate — Council (Sch 5 Pt I 2, excluding funds passed to Homes England) and County Council (Sch 5 Pt II 3, excluding the Bus Stop Contribution). Late payment by the Owner attracts the same interest rate after 7 days (cl. 11).
