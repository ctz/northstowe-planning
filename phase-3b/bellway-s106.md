## 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement the following expressions shall have the following meanings:

**"1990 Act"** means the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 as amended.

**"Affordable Dwelling"** means a Dwelling to be provided as Affordable Housing pursuant to an approved Affordable Housing Scheme in accordance with Part I of the Second Schedule unless otherwise agreed in writing with the Council.

**"Affordable Housing"** means housing for eligible persons whose housing needs are not met by the market as defined in Annex 2 of the Framework, being the Requisite Number of Affordable Dwellings.

**"Affordable Housing Land"** means the parts of the Site on which the Affordable Dwellings are to be provided and which shall accord with the definition of Clustering provided for in this Agreement.

**"Clustering"** means the provision of Affordable Dwellings in accordance with the principles in paragraphs 7–9 of Annex 10 of the Council's Greater Cambridge Housing Strategy 2019–2023 (or such updated or replacement policy in effect at the relevant time) unless otherwise agreed with the Council; and in this Development clusters of no more than 15 Affordable Dwellings shall be permitted unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Council.

## 2. Construction of this Deed

2.1. Where in this Agreement reference is made to any clause, paragraph or schedule or recital such reference (unless the context otherwise requires) is a reference to a clause, paragraph or schedule or recital in this Agreement.

2.2. Words importing the singular meaning where the context so admits include the plural meaning and vice versa.

2.3. Words of the masculine gender include the feminine and neuter genders and words denoting actual persons include companies, corporations and firms and all such words shall be construed interchangeable in that manner.

2.4. Wherever there is more than one person named as a party and where more than one party undertakes an obligation, all their obligations can be enforced against all of them jointly and against each individually unless there is an express provision otherwise.

2.5. Any reference to an Act of Parliament shall include any modification, extension or re-enactment of that Act for the time being in force and shall include all instruments, orders, plans, regulations, permissions and directions for the time being made, issued or given under that Act or deriving validity from it.

2.6. Unless expressly specified otherwise in this Agreement, reference to any party to this Agreement shall include the successors in title to that party and to any deriving title through or under that party in accordance with section 106(3) of the 1990 Act and in the case of the Council and the County Council the successors to their respective statutory functions in accordance with section 106(3) of the 1990 Act.

## 3. Legal Basis

3.1. This Agreement is made pursuant to Section 106 of the 1990 Act and to the extent that any of the obligations contained in this Agreement are not planning obligations within the meaning of the 1990 Act they are entered into pursuant to powers contained in Section 111 of the Local Government Act 1972, Section 1 of the Localism Act 2011 and all other enabling powers.

3.2. The covenants, restrictions and requirements imposed upon the Owners under this Agreement create planning obligations pursuant to Section 106 of the 1990 Act and bind the Site and are enforceable by the Council as local planning authority and where applicable by the County Council against such interest on the part of the Owners, and are entered into with the intent that subject to clauses 7.10 and 7.12 the same shall be enforceable without limit of time not only against the Owners but also against its successors in title and assigns and any person corporate or otherwise claiming through or under the Owners an interest or estate created hereafter in the Site or any part or parts thereof as if that person had also been an original covenanting party in respect of such of the covenants and undertakings which relate to the interest or estate for the time being held by that person. ## The Second Schedule — The Obligations

### Part I: Affordable Housing

**1. Criteria for Affordable Scheme**

1.1. Prior to the Commencement of Development the Owners shall submit the Affordable Housing Scheme to the Council for approval unless otherwise agreed in writing.

1.2. The housing mix of the Affordable Dwellings for the purposes of the submission of any Affordable Housing Scheme shall be based on local housing needs at the time the submission is made and shall be supported by evidence as to such local housing needs, provided that the parties agree that where there is evidence of a material change in the local housing needs (meaning a change of more than 10%) then a fresh submission may be required by the Council if a Reserved Matters application for the Affordable Housing Scheme is still to be made.

1.3. In producing the Affordable Housing Scheme the Owners shall ensure that, unless otherwise agreed by the Council, the area of any roads and sewers which are to serve the Affordable Dwellings and which are not to be the subject of agreements under section 38 of the Highways Act 1980 and section 104 of the Water Industry Act 1991 are kept to the minimum area as is reasonably possible.

**2. Registered Provider**

If the Owners wish to dispose of the Affordable Housing to a Registered Provider that has not previously been approved by the Council, the Owners may nominate such Registered Provider for approval by the Council, which approval the Council shall not unreasonably withhold or delay.

**3. Agreement with Approved Registered Provider**

3.1. The Owners shall enter into an agreement with an approved Registered Provider(s) for:

a) the sale of the Affordable Housing Land; and
b) the construction of the Affordable Dwellings

which complies with the Owners' other obligations in this Part I to the Second Schedule including the transfer of the relevant part of the Affordable Housing Land in a Clean Condition and with the provision of Services and the grant of rights over all necessary Service Installations.

3.2. Any such agreement as required by paragraph 3.1 in this Part I to the Second Schedule may be either:

a) by means of separate agreements for (i) the sale of the land and (ii) the construction of each of the Affordable Dwellings; or
b) a single agreement for the transfer of such land and completion of such Affordable Dwellings; or
c) an agreement for the sale to the Registered Provider of completed dwellings, the sale of each being completed when the Affordable Dwelling is ready for Occupation unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Registered Provider.

3.3. Any service charge payable in respect of Affordable Housing shall be kept to the minimum consistent with the limitations imposed by the Housing Act 1985 and (if required by the Council) the Affordable Housing Scheme shall include proposals by the Owners to ensure that the service charge does not exceed levels which may prevent the Affordable Housing qualifying as affordable under any guidelines or criteria of Homes England (or respective successors) current at the date when the Affordable Housing Scheme is being submitted for approval.

**4. Timing**

4.1. Unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Council:

a) the Owners will not permit more than 10% of the Market Dwellings to be Occupied unless they have first entered into an agreement or agreements with a Registered Provider for the construction and/or purchase of Affordable Housing which complies with the terms of paragraph 3 of this Part I to the Second Schedule; and
b) no more than 50% of the Market Dwellings shall be Occupied until 50% of the Affordable Dwellings have been constructed and completed ready for Occupation; and
c) no more than 80% of the Market Dwellings shall be Occupied until all of the Affordable Dwellings have been constructed and completed ready for Occupation.

**5. Occupation of Affordable Dwellings**

5.1. The Affordable Dwellings shall be Occupied only (as appropriate and depending on the tenure as determined within the approved Affordable Housing Scheme or as otherwise agreed in writing with the Council):

a) upon assured tenancies at an Affordable Rent; and/or
b) pursuant to Leases as provided herein or as may be agreed from time to time between the Council and the Registered Provider.

**6. Qualifying Persons**

6.1. Subject to priority arrangements set out in the Fourth Schedule, the Occupation of any Affordable Dwelling shall be limited to a Qualifying Person in the District of South Cambridgeshire, PROVIDED THAT after a period of 8 weeks the Registered Provider may allow persons who are not Qualifying Persons to Occupy any Dwelling if throughout such period of 8 weeks there are no Qualifying Persons available and willing to take up Occupation or an assignment of the Lease when an Affordable Dwelling becomes vacant or any Lease becomes available for assignment, and provided that such disposition is in accordance with the objects of the Registered Provider or the terms of the Lease.

**7. Nomination Rights**

7.1. The Owners covenant that the nomination arrangements as per Home Link Choice Based Lettings Policy as set out in the Fourth Schedule shall be complied with unless the Council shall agree any alternative arrangements in writing.

**8. Liability and Chargee Provisions**

## The Fourth Schedule — Nomination Rights

1. Pursuant to Part I of the Second Schedule, as between the Registered Provider and the Council, the following provisions shall govern the nominations/allocations of Occupation of the Affordable Dwellings, provided always that the Affordable Dwellings shall be allocated to Qualifying Persons with a local connection, and in relation to those with a local connection priority will be given in the following order:

a) **1st priority** — Qualifying Persons with a local connection to Longstanton; and then
b) **2nd priority** — Qualifying Persons with a local connection to either Northstowe, Swavesey, Over, Willingham, Rampton or Oakington; and then
c) **3rd priority** — Qualifying Persons in the District of South Cambridgeshire.

2. An applicant will be considered to have a local connection to the relevant village if they meet one of the following criteria:

a) The applicant has worked (paid employment) in the village for the last 12 months for sixteen hours or more per week; or
b) The applicant has lived in the village for at least 5 years out of the last 8 years; or
c) The applicant has family members who are resident in the village. Family members are defined as parents, children or brothers or sisters who have been resident in the village for a period of 5 years or longer. Other close family ties will be considered in agreement with the Council on a case by case basis; or
d) There are special circumstances that the Council considers give rise to a local connection to the village, for example where the applicant's substantive role is as a carer to a person resident in the village.

3. The Registered Provider shall, subject to paragraph 1 of this Fourth Schedule above, allocate the Affordable Rented Housing which shall become vacant and available for occupation through the 'Home-Link' Choice Based Lettings Scheme and the Council's allocations policy (or any other allocation system required by the Council at the point of letting).

4. The Registered Provider shall, subject to paragraph 1 of this Fourth Schedule above, offer the Shared Ownership Dwellings to Qualifying Persons registered with the appointed Help to Buy Agent in accordance with any Homes England guidelines (or any subsequent system required by Government at the point of resale).

5. The Registered Provider and the Council shall, subject to paragraph 1 of this Fourth Schedule above, liaise as necessary with regard to local housing need generally and the list of Qualifying Persons.

6. The Registered Provider shall provide to the Council applicant details of properties allocated.

7. The Council and the Registered Provider shall, subject to paragraph 1 of this Fourth Schedule above, have the following nomination rights in respect of the Affordable Dwellings:

| Nominations | |
|---|---|
