# Phase 3B Northstowe — Section 106 Obligations, Contributions & Triggers

Summary of the s106 Deed (Town & Country Planning Act 1990, s.106) for **Phase 3b, Northstowe** dated March 2022, between **South Cambridgeshire District Council (1)**, **Cambridgeshire County Council (2)** and **Homes and Communities Agency / Homes England (the Owner) (3)**. Application ref **20/02142/OUT**.

> **Source note:** Compiled from the two OCR'd engrossment text files (Parts 1 & 2). The OCR is heavily garbled in places, especially inside the tabular schedules. Contribution **amounts** are taken from the definitions in Part 1; **triggers** from the schedule tables in Part 2. Items flagged ⚠ are where the OCR is ambiguous and the value should be confirmed against the executed deed.

Key recurring terms:
- **Occupation** = occupation of a building (excluding construction/fit-out/marketing/security).
- **Trigger Event** = the point (usually a stated number of occupied Dwellings, or service of a Notice) by which the obligation must be performed; the Owner must not exceed the trigger until the related obligation is discharged.
- **Aggregate Dwellings** = dwellings on this Site plus Phase 2 and/or Phase 3A.
- Contributions are index-linked (BCIS / CPI / PWLB rate as noted in clause 19).
