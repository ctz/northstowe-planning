# Northstowe Phase 2 — S106 Obligations: Actions, Contributions & Triggers

Summary of every action (land transfer, construction, etc.) and financial contribution (amount + recipient) and the trigger that brings it due, taken from **Schedules 1–18** of the Phase 2 Planning Obligation Agreement.

**Key on dwelling triggers** — read carefully, the agreement uses two different counters:
- **"across the Northstowe Phase 1 Site and the Site"** = combined Phase 1 + Phase 2 dwelling count.
- **"on Phase 2" / "on the Site"** = Phase 2 dwellings only.

Unless stated otherwise, the obligated party is the **Owners** (HCA and co-owners), and every financial instalment carries the standard proviso that no dwelling may be occupied beyond the specified number until the related instalment is paid.

## Schedules 17 & 18 — Council Covenants (reciprocal)

These bind SCDC (Sch. 17) and the County Council (Sch. 18) rather than the Owners, but underpin the above:
- **Clawback** — unspent/uncommitted contributions returned 10 years after receipt (5 years for the Capital Transport Monitoring and Cycleway contributions).
- County Council to construct **Indoor Sports Building** within 18 months of receiving its contribution; to construct **MUGA** by the Secondary School's opening to pupils (subject to SCDC's £32,000 payment).
- County Council to accept Campus Site / Second Primary School Land transfers and use land solely for transferred purpose.
- SCDC to use reasonable endeavours to establish a **Town Council for Northstowe** able to accept land/building transfers.
