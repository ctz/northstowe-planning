# Phase 3a Northstowe — Section 106 Deed: Actions, Contributions & Triggers

Source: `20_02171_OUT-PHASE_3A_SECTION_106_ENGROSSMENT` Parts 1 & 2 (deed dated 25 March 2022).
Parties: South Cambridgeshire District Council (1), Cambridgeshire County Council (2), Homes and Communities Agency = "the Owner" (3).

**Conventions used below**
- "Occ N" = Occupation of N Dwellings (Total Dwellings unless stated; "Aggregate" = Site + Phase 2 dwellings).
- "WD" = Working Days. "Notice" triggers are payable a set number of WD after the Council serves the relevant notice.
- Amounts are the headline figures from the Definitions; instalment values in brackets are computed from the stated percentage.
- ⚠ flags where the OCR is ambiguous and the figure should be checked against the original deed.

### Notes / OCR caveats
- ⚠ **Community Chest** 2nd instalment: OCR reads "Occupation of 4,000"; pattern with the Community Development Officer Contribution strongly implies **Occ 1,000**. Verify against the original.
- ⚠ **Secondary Education Contribution** instalments read 10% / 65% / 35% in the OCR (sums to 110%); by analogy with the Primary School contributions the final instalment is most likely **25%**. Verify.
- The "Clause 19" table in the deed is only an **indexation** table (it sets the index — BCIS / PWLB / CPI — for each Contribution); the amounts above come from the Definitions section.
- "Total Dwellings" vs "Aggregate Dwellings" (Site + Phase 2) vs "Phase 3 Dwellings" distinctions are preserved where the deed draws them, notably for the junction works.
