# Northstowe Phase 1 — S106 Obligations: Actions, Contributions & Triggers

Summary of every action (land transfer, construction, etc.) and financial contribution (amount + recipient) and the trigger that brings it due, taken from **Schedules 1–18** of the Phase 1 Planning Obligation by Agreement (dated **22 April 2014**, between Gallagher Longstanton Ltd, SCDC, Cambridgeshire County Council, the Other Landowners and JJ Gallagher Ltd as guarantor).

> Source: `README.md` in this folder — an LLM conversion of the original PDF. Refer to the original document before relying on anything here.

**Key on dwelling triggers** — Phase 1 is the outline permission for **up to 1,500 dwellings**. Unlike the Phase 2 agreement (which uses a combined "Phase 1 + Site" counter), every dwelling threshold here is a **Phase 1 dwelling count** ("Occupation of the Nth Dwelling", "Completion of N Dwellings"). Triggers distinguish:
- **"Occupation"** — a dwelling brought into use.
- **"Completion"** — a dwelling built to practical completion (used for the Secondary School Construction instalments).
- **"Implementation" / "Commencement"** — the start of works (Commencement excludes demolition, site clearance, remediation, drainage/services, temporary access, etc. per clause 1).

**Overall cap (clause 29) — the defining feature of Phase 1.** The Owners' total liability for monetary Contributions and Works in Kind is **capped at £30 million** (excluding affordable housing, which sits outside the cap — recital G, clause 29). Once the cap is reached, the Owners' liability for outstanding Contributions and Works in Kind ceases (the "Remaining Obligations"), and SCDC / the County Council must use Reasonable Endeavours to fund those from other sources (Schedules 17 & 18). Certain items are excluded from the cap calculation — notably the prudential-borrowing interest charge under Schedule 2 (clause 29.1.9) and any Owner overspend above the Allocated 106 Costs (clause 29.1.8). A **clawback** mechanism applies where overall savings reach at least **£3,275,500** (before Indexation): unexpended/uncommitted balances are returned to the Owners with interest after the relevant holding period.

Unless stated otherwise, the obligated party is the **Owners** (Gallagher and co-owners), and each financial instalment carries the standard proviso that no further dwelling may be Occupied beyond the specified number until the related instalment is paid. Contributions are subject to Indexation (BCIS) per clause 23 unless noted.

## Schedules 17 & 18 — Council Covenants (reciprocal)

These bind SCDC (Sch. 17) and the County Council (Sch. 18) rather than the Owners, but underpin the above:

- **Clawback** — once the £30m cap is reached and overall savings total at least **£3,275,500** (before Indexation), unexpended/uncommitted balances are returned to the payor with interest. Holding period before clawback is generally **10 years** after receipt, but **5 years** for the County Council's Off Site Footpath & Cycleway Contribution and Primary School Building Construction Contribution.
- **Clawback reporting** — SCDC and the County Council each notify the Owners every 6 months (from 2 years after Commencement) of sums held, origin, unexpended amounts and Remaining Obligations discharged, plus a final notice within 40 Working Days of the last Remaining Obligation being discharged.
- **County Council** to construct, complete and open the **Primary School Building** to pupils by the Delivery Date (3 full academic terms after the Primary School Land Transfer); to accept the Primary School Land / HWRC Land transfers and use them solely for the transferred purpose; to draw on Owner Bonds only on Owner default.
- **SCDC** to use Reasonable Endeavours to establish/approve a **Town Council for Northstowe** able to accept land/building transfers; to put a community development worker in post ≥9 months before the Community Building opens; to accept the Community Building / Allotment / Sports Pavilion / Open Space land transfers on each Schedule's terms.
- Both councils to apply Contributions only for their stated purpose, and to work with the Owners (Reasonable Endeavours) to secure external funding/grants/credits for the Development.
